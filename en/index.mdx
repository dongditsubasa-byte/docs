## About us

**Nanjing SunnyMeteo Technology** (小晴天科技) delivers high-precision global weather, climate, and environmental data. Guided by *“Weather you can count on”*, we package multi-source fusion forecasts and AI capabilities as APIs, MCP, and SDKs for developers, enterprises, and AI applications.

We aim to be a trusted partner for meteorological and environmental intelligence—bringing weather science from specialized domains into everyday products and decisions that support carbon neutrality, smart cities, and sustainable development in the AI era.

## Products

| Product line | What you get |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Weather API** | City conditions, 24h/360h and 15-day forecasts, life index, domestic & global alerts |
| **Air quality** | AQI conditions and hourly forecast |
| **Station API** | PV/wind station CRUD, satellite irradiance, hourly and 15-min weather/power forecasts |
| **Disaster API** | China/global earthquake catalogs, global & China typhoon catalogs and track detail |
| **Data dictionary** | Weather codes, Beaufort scale, city codes, units and coordinate conventions |

See [API usage](/en/api-guide) for access details, or open the [API Explorer](https://api.sunnymeteo.com/docs).
